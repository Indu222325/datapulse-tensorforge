API

The service follows the TensorForge 2.0 Phase 2 contract. Every endpoint except /health needs the key as X-API-Key or Authorization: Bearer.

CallWhat it does
GET /healthPublic. 200 when the model is loaded, 503 while it loads.
POST /predictOne ticket in, one prediction out.
POST /predict/batch1 to 100 tickets, answered in the same order. One bad item fails the whole request.
POST /batch/jobs1 to 5,000 tickets as a background job. Answers 202 with a job id.
GET /batch/jobs/{id}Job status and progress.
GET /batch/jobs/{id}/resultsPredictions once the job has succeeded. Supports offset and limit.
DELETE /batch/jobs/{id}Cancel a job or discard its results.
